Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 13, 2026

EdgarWolf ("we", "us") is operated by Jason Ostergren. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

Information We Collect

  • Email address — collected when you subscribe (via Stripe) or when you use the "Restore Access" flow. Used to deliver your subscription, send welcome emails, and (for Pro+ subscribers) filing alert emails.
  • Payment information — handled entirely by Stripe. We never see or store your card number. We receive a Stripe customer ID and subscription status.
  • Watchlist data — companies you add to your watchlist are stored server-side for Pro and Pro+ subscribers. Standard users' watchlists are stored only in your browser's localStorage.
  • Usage events — we log anonymized events (e.g., "company searched", "upgrade modal opened") to Railway application logs. No personally identifiable information is attached to these events.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain your subscription
  • To send welcome emails and filing alert emails (Pro+ only, M–F 8 AM–6 PM ET)
  • To restore access to your subscription across devices
  • To understand how the product is being used (aggregate, anonymized)

Data We Do Not Collect

We do not use tracking pixels, third-party analytics (no Google Analytics, no Meta Pixel), or advertising cookies. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with any third party for marketing purposes.

Third-Party Services

  • Stripe — payment processing. Stripe Privacy Policy
  • Resend — transactional email delivery. Resend Privacy Policy
  • Railway — application hosting. Application logs (including event logs) are retained per Railway's standard log retention policy.
  • SEC EDGAR — all financial data displayed is sourced from publicly available SEC filings.

Data Storage and Security

User and watchlist data is stored in a SQLite database on a Railway persistent volume. We use HTTPS for all connections. We do not store passwords — authentication is handled entirely through Stripe subscription verification.

Data Retention and Deletion

You can cancel your subscription at any time via the Stripe Customer Portal (the "Manage" button in the app header). To request deletion of your account data (email, watchlist), email jason@edgarwolf.com and we will delete it within 30 days.

Cookies and Local Storage

We use browser localStorage to store your subscription tier, Stripe session ID, and watchlist (Standard tier). We do not use tracking cookies. No consent banner is shown because we do not set any non-essential cookies.

Children's Privacy

EdgarWolf is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy occasionally. Material changes will be noted by updating the "Last updated" date above.

Contact

Questions? Email jason@edgarwolf.com.
